Twelve skills in one paste. Plus one more.

Each big skill runs a full workflow and pulls in its mini skills only when it needs them, so your agent stays fast. Click any skill to see what it does, when to use it and what's inside.

It doesn't grade its own homework.

Super Design, Landing Page and Review share one rubric and one rule: the agent that builds the page never scores it.

  1. Shoot itDesktop and phone screenshots, plus a check for anything spilling off the side of the screen.
  2. A fresh judge scores itTen weighted dimensions with fixed anchors, and hard caps. A broken phone layout caps the page at 5/10.
  3. Critique, then fix worst firstEvery issue cites a screenshot and comes with a concrete fix. Critical issues go first.
  4. Repeat until 8/10It stops at 8.0 with nothing below 7. If the score stalls, it offers two new directions instead of polishing in circles.
